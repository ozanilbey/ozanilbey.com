Waplog is a **dating platform** for people who are on a quest to find their soulmate. It has **over 90 million members** from more than 50 countries. It provides over 20 universal languages.

I designed a generic multiplatform user interface for Waplog Mobile **in 2010**. The same design is used in all mobile platforms (iOS, Android and mobile web).

> I also worked on user interface design and frontend development of [Waplog Web](/works/waplog-web-application) in 2012.
