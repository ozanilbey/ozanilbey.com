SOCRadar is a **digital risk protection platform** providing contextualized intelligence.

I **designed the SOCRadar website**. Then I heavily **customized a WordPress theme** (HTML + CSS + JS + graphics) to match the design and also accommodate the client’s needs.

> I also worked on [SOCRadar Branding](/works/socradar-branding){/* and created [SOCRadar Documents](/works/socradar-documents) */}.

> Special thanks to [Ceren](https://cerenmurgan.com) for her significant contribution to this project!
