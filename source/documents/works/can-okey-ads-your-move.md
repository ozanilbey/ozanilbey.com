Can Okey is a **social game**, based on traditional Turkish game Okey, developed and managed by Digitoy Games. It is available on Apple Store, Google Play Store, and Facebook.

I designed advertisement visuals for Can Okey **in 2017**. All images are used on Facebook as sponsored content.

> I also designed [Can Okey Facebook Page](/works/can-okey-facebook-page).

> Special thanks to [Ceren](https://cerenmurgan.com) for her significant contribution to this project!
