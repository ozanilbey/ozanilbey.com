Okey Extra is a **social game**, based on traditional Turkish game Okey, developed and managed by Digitoy Games. It is available on Apple Store, Google Play Store, and Facebook.

I designed 8 different Facebook post templates for Okey Extra **in 2016**. Each of these templates serves different purposes.

> I also designed Facebook Pages for the other games developed by Digitoy Games such as [101 Extra](/works/101-extra-facebook-page), [Can Okey](/works/can-okey-facebook-page), [Tekel Okey](/works/tekel-okey-facebook-page), and [Rakkip](/works/rakkip-facebook-page).

> Special thanks to [Ceren](https://cerenmurgan.com) for her significant contribution to this project!
