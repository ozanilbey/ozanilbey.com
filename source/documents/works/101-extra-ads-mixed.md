101 Extra is a **social game**, based on traditional Turkish game Okey, developed and managed by Digitoy Games. It is available on Apple Store, Google Play Store, and Facebook.

I designed more than 50 advertisement visuals for 101 Extra **in 2016**. All images are used on Facebook as sponsored content.

> I also designed [101 Extra Facebook Page](/works/101-extra-facebook-page).

> Special thanks to [Ceren](https://cerenmurgan.com) for her significant contribution to this project!
